## Bio

Jack is a Data Scientist and Engineer with 10+ years of experience building data products people actually use, from internal analytics platforms, to production-grade, client-facing ML and AI data products. He focusees on trend forecasting in retail and e-commerce, where data is messy, questions are ambiguous, and everyone would like an answer _yesterday_.

His computer science background led him to become the the optim-<u>_ist_</u> and optim-<u>_iser_</u> he is today, applying both principles to everything from fitness and travel to [Navigating the AI-pocalypse](hugo-ref:portfolio/2026-01-23-analytics-engineering-technical-test-travails-with-dbt-and-duckdb.md).

[Redundancy in October 2025](hugo-ref:portfolio/2026-01-23-analytics-engineering-technical-test-travails-with-dbt-and-duckdb.md) was the catalyst to [building this site](hugo-ref:portfolio/2026-01-23-analytics-engineering-technical-test-travails-with-dbt-and-duckdb.md) as part notebook, part portfolio, and part evidence that side projects can come to fruition.

## How I work

I think in terms of systems, constraints, and trade-offs, shaped mostly by my degree but also by working in an industry where decisions are fast, stakes are high, and feedback loops can be slow. I focus on building platforms that reduce cognitive load and create leverage — making the _right way_ the easy way.

###### Leadership style

Jack aligns most strongly with **servant leadership**; not as a philosophy but a practical strategy to create a multiplier effect. He doesn't believe in choosing between technical and managerial tracks. Technical leadership requires both and so far he's spent his career staying hands-on while building and leading teams.

###### Traits of the best

The best engineers and data scientists I’ve had the pleasure of working with tend to:

- Run towards hard problems
- Understand _why_ before acting
- Take ownership of fixing problems
- Leave things in a better state than they were found
- Communicate clearly, often, and adapted to their audience
- Invest in people around them

###### Some rules to live by

- Impact over elegance
- Simplicity over complexity
- Long-term maintainability over short-term optimisation
